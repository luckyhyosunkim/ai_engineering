The idea in 4 lines

1. FrontierCells we know about but haven't visited yet. Starts with just A.
2. Take one outQueue takes the oldest (BFS), Stack takes the newest (DFS).
3. Goal? Done.Otherwise mark it explored and add its non-wall, unseen neighbors (up, down, left, right).
4. Trace backEach Node remembers its parent, so from B we walk back to A for the path.
